These are a handful of IDD-Lite validation frames and two clips, included only
so the demonstration has something to open on arrival.

The dataset itself is not redistributed. IDD is released by IIIT Hyderabad
through registration, with its licence presented at download time:

    https://idd.insaan.iiit.ac.in/

To reproduce any measurement, register, download IDD-Lite, and verify your copy:

    python tools/fetch_dataset.py --archive <the archive you downloaded>
